**Dear Worthy Brother Knights:**

My name is Kevin Devlin, and I am your new Grand Knight for the 2026/27 Columbian year. I am humbled to be your new Grand Knight. I would like to thank Pat Power, as our now Past Grand Knight, for his all his time to our council but for his guidance to me.

As Knights our mission, grounded on the principles of Charity, Unity, Fraternity and Patriotism, calls us to be active stewards of our Family, Church and Community. Our founder, a Catholic priest, Blessed Michael J. McGivney created as a mutual benefit a society to protect Catholic families, support widows and orphans and keep Catholic men anchored to their faith. Has grown from one parish in 1882 to 17 countries today.

Our council is involved in many activities. Our monthly meeting, 3rd Wednesday of the month, is one way to meet other Brothers. But if family or work commitments are an issue, our PR guy Geno, sends out regular emails of up coming events. Please read them. 

Please make sure your email address is up to date and/or your phone number. Just one other example is the Pancake Breakfast, 3rd Sunday of the month, great way to meet other Brothers.

Personally, for me, I didn’t really know anyone when I first joined. I learned that you are not alone, there is always support to help. The friendships that have happened, I am truly blessed.

The Knights of Columbus has been around for 144 years, and our St. Peter council for 48 years has happened with Brothers not sitting on our hands but by holding hands and giving our time, talent and treasure.

Vivat Jesus,
